# Setup

## Overview

A first launch opens a welcome screen that creates your remi account. The account holds a balance of transcription hours, and each recording you transcribe draws on it.

## Create Your Account

1. Enter your name and your email.
2. Pick your province or territory.
3. Enter a promo code if you have one. A code adds free hours to the account.
4. Press *Get started*.

A welcome message confirms the account and any promo code it applied. The balance shows in the sidebar. A transcription takes the length of its recording from the balance, rounded up to the second, and notes, rewrites, and questions take nothing.

## Buy Hours

1. Press *Buy hours* in the sidebar, or on the *Account* tab of Settings.
2. Type how many hours you want. The card shows the price and the balance you will have.
3. Press *Buy hours* on the card, then pay on the same page.

The hours land in your balance once the payment goes through, and a message shows the new balance. A purchase is a one-time payment, so nothing renews.

The *Promo code* box on the *Account* tab redeems a code later. Each code works once per email address, and an email address gets one offer from each event, whichever of the event's codes it uses.

## Sign In On Another Computer

1. Press *Copy sign-in code* on the *Account* tab of Settings, on a computer that is signed in.
2. Press *Have a sign-in code?* on the welcome screen of the other computer.
3. Paste the code and press *Sign in*.

The code opens your account, so keep it private, like a password.

## What Leaves Your Computer

The audio and the names on your People page go to the transcription service. The transcript, the template, and the people you tick go to the notes service, which also handles corrections, rewrites, and titles. A question on the *Ask* page goes to the notes service with excerpts from the recordings that match it. Both services sit behind remi's server.

The server passes each request on to the AI service and keeps only the length and a fingerprint of each piece of audio, so a recording is billed once.
