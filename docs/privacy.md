# Privacy Policy

## Overview

Stratus Advanced Technologies makes remi, a desktop app that turns a recording of a meeting into notes, and runs its website at remirememberthat.com. This policy describes the personal information the app and the website collect, why they collect it, who else receives it, how long it is kept, and how you can see, correct, or delete it. This version took effect on November 19, 2026.

## Contact

Each question, request, or complaint about your personal information goes to the Privacy Officer at [info@stratusadv.com](mailto:info@stratusadv.com). The Privacy Officer is accountable for how Stratus Advanced Technologies handles personal information, including its handling by the service providers outside Canada listed under [Service Providers](#service-providers).

## The Website

### Google Analytics

The website uses Google Analytics, loaded through Google Tag Manager, to learn how people find the site and which pages they read. Each page loads them when it opens, and a banner on your first visit tells you so and offers *Decline* to turn them off. Google Analytics records the pages you view, how far you scroll, the links you follow to other sites, the files you download, and the forms you use. Its records also hold your browser, your device, and an approximate location that Google derives from your IP address. Google Analytics never receives your name or email address.

Google Analytics keeps two cookies in your browser:

| Cookie | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| `_ga` | A cookie that tells one browser from another. The cookie lasts up to two years after your last visit. |
| `_ga_GYQQ1H2S7Z` | A cookie that holds the state of your current visit. The cookie lasts up to two years after your last visit. |

Google's page [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) describes what Google does with this data. The *Cookie settings* link at the bottom of each page changes your choice at any time. A decline stops Google Analytics and deletes its cookies, and the website works the same either way. Your browser's local storage keeps the choice, which never leaves your browser. The website doesn't respond to a browser's Do Not Track signal.

### Click and Download Counts

The website counts clicks without cookies, and each count is a daily total that holds nothing about who clicked. The home page counts clicks on its download and sharing buttons, and a campaign page, such as the conference offer, counts each arrival. A download counts toward Windows, macOS, or Linux and toward its campaign. A download through the link in your conference offer email also records the time of your first download against your personal promo code.

### Conference Offer

The conference page asks for your first name, last name, and email address to send you a personal promo code and a download link. Your name and email address are kept with the code. Twilio SendGrid delivers the email and records whether it is opened and which of its links are clicked.

### Hosting and Fonts

GitHub hosts the website and the app's downloads, so it receives your IP address and browser details with each page and file you request. The pages also ask GitHub for remi's latest release and repository details. The home, download, and conference pages load their fonts from Google Fonts, which receives the same details. The documentation pages remember your light or dark theme choice in your browser's local storage, and the choice never leaves your browser.

## The App

### Your Account

The welcome screen asks for your name, email address, and province or territory, and for a promo code if you have one, to create your remi account. The province or territory is asked because remi is offered only in Canada. The account holds these details, the campaign of any promo code you redeem, and your balance of transcription hours. The server keeps only a hash of your sign-in code, so the code can't be read back from it.

### Purchases

Stripe processes each purchase in a checkout inside the app. Your card details go to Stripe and never reach Stratus Advanced Technologies. Stripe receives your email address, your account ID, and the hours you buy, and it collects details about your device in the checkout to detect fraud. Your account keeps the amount, currency, and hours of each purchase, with Stripe's reference numbers for it.

### Recordings, Transcripts, and Notes

Your recordings, transcripts, notes, templates, and People page stay on your computer. A transcription sends the audio and the names on your People page to the transcription service. The notes service receives the transcript, the template, and the people you tick, and it also handles corrections, rewrites, and titles. A question on the *Ask* page goes to the notes service with excerpts from the recordings that match it.

Both services run on servers that Stratus Advanced Technologies operates, and neither one stores what it receives. remi's server passes each request on to them and never keeps its content. The server keeps the length and a fingerprint of each piece of audio, so a recording is billed once, and the time and size of each question, which pace how often the *Ask* page can be used.

### Feature Counts

The app tells remi's server each time you open an audio or video file to transcribe, start a recording, start a chat, ask a question on the *Ask* page, create a folder or a template, or change the theme, colour scheme, or mode. A question also names whether it covered one meeting, a folder, or all of your meetings. The server adds each one to that day's count for the feature, and the count holds nothing about you or your account.

### Updates

The app checks GitHub for new versions and downloads them from there, so GitHub receives your IP address.

### Server Records

remi's server logs each request with its time, path, and result, and with your account ID for a request from the app. DigitalOcean, which hosts the server, receives the IP address of each request, and the server keeps a count of recent requests from each address in memory to block floods of requests. Stratus Advanced Technologies also counts sign-ups, sign-ups through each campaign, the personal promo codes each campaign page sends, promo code redemptions, purchases, and transcribed meetings and hours for its internal reports. Each of these counts is a daily total, like the counts from the website and the app, and holds no account ID, promo code, name, or email address.

## Service Providers

These companies process personal information for remi:

| Provider | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| DigitalOcean | The host of remi's server and its account database, both in Toronto, Canada. |
| GitHub | The host of the website, the app's downloads, and its updates, with servers in the United States and other countries. |

Any information stored outside Canada is subject to the laws of the country it is stored in, including access by that country's courts and authorities.

## Retention

Each kind of information is kept only as long as its purpose needs.

| Information | Retention |
|-------------|-----------|
| Account details | The details are kept while your account is open. When the account closes, your name and email address are deleted. |
| Purchase records | The records are kept for six years after the end of the year of each purchase, as Canadian tax law requires. |
| Conference offer details | Your name and email address are kept with your code until you ask for them to be deleted. |
| Recordings, transcripts, and notes | These stay on your computer until you delete them. |
| Google Analytics data | Google keeps the detailed data for up to 14 months. |

## Your Rights

Email the Privacy Officer to see the personal information Stratus Advanced Technologies holds about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to close your account. Each request gets an answer within 30 days. Your consent can be withdrawn the same way at any time. An account can't run without a name and email address, so withdrawing consent for them closes the account.

A complaint the Privacy Officer doesn't resolve can go to the [Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta](https://oipc.ab.ca) or the [Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada](https://www.priv.gc.ca).
