Private meeting notes

Your meetings, remembered.

Remi remembers every meeting you record. Ask about any of them, and it tells you what was said and which meeting it came from. It also writes up the notes in your team’s format.

Private by design

Never stored in the cloud.

Recordings are processed on our own private servers and aren’t kept afterward. The audio, transcripts, and notes stay on your computer.

Private servers Stored: nothing

The details

More than a transcript.

  • Your template Set up your format once, and every set of notes follows it.
  • Names spelled correctly Add the people you meet with, and Remi will spell their names correctly every time.
  • Easy corrections If Remi mishears a name, correct it once and the whole transcript is updated.
  • Any recording Use audio or video from your phone, a voice recorder, or a video call. You can add several files at once.
  • Long meetings Remi handles recordings up to six hours long.
  • Word, PDF, or Markdown Share the notes in whichever format your team prefers.
