# Exporting

## Overview

Press *Export* on the Notes step and pick a format.

| Format | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| *PDF* | The app opens the system print dialog. Choose *Save as PDF* as the printer. |
| *Word* | The app saves a `.docx` file. |
| *Markdown* | The app saves a `.md` file. |

## What An Export Holds

An export holds the notes and nothing else. The title, the participants, the transcript, and the audio stay out unless your template puts them on the page.

An export removes stray time markers such as `[1:23]`, so write a real time as `(9:30)` or `at 9:30`. The saved notes keep their text.
