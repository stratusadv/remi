# Notes

## Overview

The Notes step turns the transcript and a template into a document you can edit. The people you tick help the AI spell names and tell speakers apart.

## Add People

1. Open *People* in the sidebar and press *Add person*.
2. Enter a first name, plus a last name, a role, and a description if you have them.
3. Press *Save*.

A group ticks several people at once. Press *Add group*, name it, and add members with *+ Add*.

## Write Notes

1. Check the title on the Meeting step and pick a template.
2. Tick everyone who was there under *In the meeting*. The AI treats this as the complete list.
3. Tick anyone who came up but was not there under *Mentioned*.
4. Press *Next*, then *Generate notes*.
5. Edit the result. The editor saves after each change.
6. Press *Done*.

The *Regenerate notes* button replaces the whole document, including your edits, so it asks first.

## Rewrite Text

Select text and press *AI* on the toolbar, then pick an action or type your own instruction. Select nothing to rewrite the whole document.
