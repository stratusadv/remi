# Notes

## Overview

The Notes step turns the transcript and a template into a document you can edit. The people you tick help the AI spell names and tell speakers apart.

## Add People

1. Open *People* in the sidebar and press *Add person*.
2. Enter a first name, plus a last name, a role, and a description if you have them.
3. Press *Save*.

A group ticks several people at once. Press *Add group*, name it, and add members with *+ Add*.

## Write Notes

1. Check the title on the Meeting step and pick a template.
2. Tick everyone who was there under *In the meeting*. The AI treats this as the complete list.
3. Tick anyone who came up but was not there under *Mentioned*.
4. Press *Next*, then *Generate notes*.
5. Edit the result. The editor saves after each change.
6. Press *Done*.

The *Regenerate notes* button replaces the whole document, including your edits, so it asks first.

## Rewrite Text

Select text and press *AI* on the toolbar, then pick an action or type your own instruction. Select nothing to rewrite the whole document.

## Ask About Past Meetings

1. Open *Ask* in the sidebar.
2. Type a question, such as `Did Hannah agree to the renewal?`, and press *Enter*.
3. Press a meeting under the answer to open it.

The app searches the titles, people, notes, and transcripts of your recordings, and the notes service answers from the passages it found alone. A time in the question, such as *Thursday* or *last month*, favours the meetings from then. A follow-up in the same chat can lean on the questions before it, such as `What did she ask for in return?`.

The *Ask about this meeting* item in a meeting's menu and the *Ask about this folder* item in a folder's menu limit the search to that meeting or folder. Each chat is saved under *History* on the Ask page, so you can come back to it later.

With remi's own notes service, Ask answers questions for 30 days after each recording remi transcribes. A burst of questions, or a very busy month, makes remi space questions a few minutes apart: the page says how long the wait is and answers once the turn comes. A note under an answer says when half of the month's full-speed questions are used.
